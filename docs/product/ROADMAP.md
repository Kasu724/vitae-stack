# Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Local foundation

M01-M03 establish pinned tooling, SQLite migrations, the trusted local principal/workspace, loopback security, and the test harness.

## Phase 1 — Verified career data

M04-M07 deliver the Career Information Bank, document/version primitives, safe import, and extraction approval. No unapproved extraction can enter generation context.

## Phase 2 — Job evidence and providers

M08-M12 deliver text/file/secure-URL job ingestion, reviewed requirements, evidence matching, and optional Ollama/OpenAI-compatible adapters.

## Phase 3 — Grounded documents

M13-M18 deliver grounded resume/cover-letter tailoring, suggestion review, manual editing, deterministic ATS estimates, export, portability, and deletion.

## Phase 4 — Local release

M19-M22 complete abuse testing, end-to-end/recovery checks, performance verification, and the Windows x64 portable artifact with bundled runtime and local-data persistence.

## After MVP

OCR, additional locale-specific ATS rules, semantic retrieval, more provider adapters, and safe template extensions may be evaluated separately. Remote identity, shared workspaces, and hosted/operator scaling require a new product decision and are not implicit next steps. Experimental work must provide evaluation evidence and cannot bypass grounding or security gates.

The milestone graph and validation details are authoritative in [MVP implementation](../exec-plans/active/MVP_IMPLEMENTATION.md).
