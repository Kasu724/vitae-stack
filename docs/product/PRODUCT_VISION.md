# Product vision

Vitae Stack helps job seekers turn a verified record of their own experience into targeted resumes and cover letters without surrendering control of their data or letting a model invent credentials.

The product's durable advantage is not prose generation. It is the evidence chain: imports become proposals, people approve facts, job requirements map to evidence, changes remain reviewable, and every factual claim can be traced after editing and versioning.

## Outcomes

- Reduce repetitive tailoring while preserving factual accuracy.
- Make gaps and tradeoffs visible instead of hiding them behind a score.
- Produce portable, editable documents and a portable Career Information Bank.
- Run as a downloaded local tool without application or cloud accounts.
- Let provider choice change without changing product semantics or data ownership.

## Product tiers

- **MVP:** downloadable local-only, no-account tool; one automatically provisioned workspace; structured CIB; imports with approval; text/file/secure-URL jobs; grounded resume and cover-letter tailoring; manual editing; AI ATS estimates with optional job context; versions; PDF/DOCX/Markdown/text/JSON export; full data export/deletion.
- **Optional integrations:** Ollama or an explicitly configured OpenAI-compatible model endpoint.
- **Post-MVP:** OIDC, collaboration, OCR, multilingual rules, semantic embeddings, template marketplace, mobile-native clients.
- **Experimental:** privacy-preserving local embeddings, diff-aware model reranking, and reproducible layout optimization. Experimental features cannot alter factual-safety gates.

Success is measured by supported-claim rate, user correction rate, import approval accuracy, successful exports, task completion, and deletion reliability—not application volume or hiring outcomes.
