# ATS limitations

- Employers use different parsers, taxonomies, configurations, knockout questions, recruiter workflows, and human judgment. Vitae Stack has no access to them.
- A readable export can still be interpreted differently by a specific system, especially multi-column layouts, unusual fonts, non-Latin scripts, or scanned PDFs.
- Job descriptions may be incomplete, aspirational, duplicated, discriminatory, or stale; reviewed requirements reflect the supplied snapshot.
- Keyword and alignment rules cannot establish real competence. Evidence links improve traceability but are not employer verification.
- Scores are not comparable across ruleset major versions, jobs, locales, or materially different templates without context.
- English/US fixtures dominate MVP; locale-specific date, language, and accessibility behavior may be unevaluated and must be labeled.
- Content quality heuristics encode limited conventions and should not erase an individual's voice.
- No score predicts screening, interview, offer, compensation, or legal compliance.

Reports always display ruleset version, input hashes/timestamps, evaluated coverage, unavailable checks, and this limitation summary. Product copy must say “estimated ATS compatibility” and must not use “pass,” “guaranteed,” or proprietary ATS logos without permission.
