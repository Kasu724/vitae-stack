# ATS limitations

- Employers use different parsers, taxonomies, configurations, knockout questions, recruiter workflows, and human judgment. Vitae Stack has no access to them.
- A readable export can still be interpreted differently by a specific system, especially multi-column layouts, unusual fonts, non-Latin scripts, or scanned PDFs.
- Job descriptions may be incomplete, aspirational, duplicated, discriminatory, or stale; analysis reflects only the supplied snapshot.
- AI keyword and alignment judgments cannot establish real competence. Evidence links remain separate traceability and are not employer verification.
- Model output can be inconsistent, biased, incomplete, or wrong. Scores are not comparable across providers, models, task versions, jobs, locales, or materially different templates.
- English/US examples dominate MVP testing; model behavior for other languages and locales is not calibrated.
- AI style suggestions should not erase an individual's voice.
- No score predicts screening, interview, offer, compensation, or legal compliance.

Reports display provider, model, task version, input/output hashes, timestamps, optional-job status, and this limitation summary. Product copy must say “AI ATS estimate” or “estimated ATS compatibility” and must not use “pass,” “guaranteed,” or proprietary ATS logos without permission.
