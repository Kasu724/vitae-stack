# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Vitae Stack harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful and specific; critique ideas and code rather than people; accept responsibility and correct mistakes; protect private career and security information; and prioritize the community's health over individual advantage. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized attention, threats, doxxing, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, or publishing another person's private information.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers using the contact route published in the repository's GitHub Security tab or, until one is configured, by opening a neutral maintainer-contact request that contains no sensitive details. Maintainers will acknowledge reports, protect confidentiality where possible, investigate conflicts of interest, and apply proportionate responses: correction, warning, temporary restriction, or permanent removal. False reports made in good faith are not violations.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project. It is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1; enforcement decisions remain subject to applicable law and platform rules.
